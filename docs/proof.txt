what has been proved, what is still open, and which proof gate owns each claim.

`tools/check_playability_gap_ledger.py`. The `POST_PLAYABLE_GAP[...]` rows
below are the next hardware/runtime claim boundaries that must remain open after
scripted playable Doom until their own gates produce machine-checkable evidence.


`doomfaultip=<eip>`, `doomfaultv=<vector>`, `doomfaulterr=<error-code>`, and
the compact `fault=` frame. Normal runs require `doomexit`, `doomfault`,
`doomfaultip`, `doomfaultv`, and `doomfaulterr` to stay zero.

The FAT16 image has root entries for `DEFAULT.CFG` and `DOOMSAV0.DSG` through
`DOOMSAV5.DSG`. Reboot comparison now requires the fresh baseline, the
after-write snapshot, and status evidence that Doom closed the save file before
the mutated image is inspected.

Hardware boundary: UEFI, PCI enumeration, AHCI, USB, SMP, APIC, HPET, and
physical hardware remain unclaimed. `UEFI_BOOT[...]`,
`UEFI_HOST_ARTIFACT[...]`, `PCI_STATUS[...]`, `PCI_TABLE[...]`, `pciprobe=`,
and `pcitabcap=` are status/proof-boundary fields only until a hardware support
row claims more. `pcicons=OK`,
`pcilookide=`, and `pcilookahci=` add a status-only kernel consumer proof that
the read-only PCI table can be queried for storage-class intent; they do not
claim AHCI, driver binding, or broad PCI support.
